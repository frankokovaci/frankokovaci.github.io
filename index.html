How much does Italy's impatriate regime really save?

Question
A company is moving an employee to Milan. What will they take home, and how much does the impatriate regime save compared with standard taxation?
What I built
A unit-tested Python tax engine using public 2025 and 2026 Italian rules (IRPEF, INPS, employee deductions, regional and municipal surcharges), plus a scenario generator feeding a Power BI what-if report.
Finding
At €80,000 gross, the regime cuts the effective tax rate from 31% to 10%, worth about €16,800 a year, or roughly €84,000 over five years.

Did wages keep up with food prices in the Western Balkans?

Question
Since 2015, have average wages in Albania, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Montenegro and Serbia kept pace with the price of bread, meat, milk, eggs and oil, and how does each compare with the EU?
What I built
An end-to-end Fabric pipeline: Eurostat API ingestion, a bronze–silver–gold Lakehouse with data-quality checks, a star schema and a Direct Lake semantic model.
Key metric
Food affordability index = wage index ÷ food price index × 100. Above 100, wages grew faster than food prices.

SELECT delivery_bucket,
       ROUND(AVG(r.review_score), 2) AS avg_review,
       ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE r.review_score <= 2)

Can payroll, ledger and tax data be reconciled automatically?

Problem
Multinationals pay mobile employees through several payroll systems. Each month, finance teams reconcile payroll, general ledger and tax withholding by hand, and errors surface months later in tax filings.
What I'm building
A reconciliation engine on realistic synthetic data with known, injected errors. dbt models match records across three sources, data contracts and tests guard every load, a statistical layer flags anomalies, Apache Airflow runs the monthly close end to end, and GitHub Actions runs the test suite on every change.
How it's measured
Because the injected errors are known, the engine is scored on how many it catches and how many false alarms it raises, the same way an audit would test a control.

Which large US companies are under margin pressure?

Question
Using official filings, which S&P 500 companies have seen operating margins shrink over the last five years, and is the cause rising costs or slowing revenue?
What I'm building
Incremental ingestion of company financial data from the free SEC EDGAR API, a mapping layer that standardises inconsistent accounting tags into one income statement, ratio and variance models, and a Power BI report with deployment pipelines from dev to production.
Why it's hard
Companies report the same line item under different tags and restate prior years. Standardising this reliably is the real work, and the kind of problem finance data teams face every day.
